# App artwork

`Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png` is the exact icon previously
used by the PS2 iOS app from 17 October 2026, recovered from its local Git blob
`ed367fd6dd2fb4763aa9b62f810523fbb9b82524`. The PS2 repository cleanup on
20 October replaced it with the orange typographic mark.

The earlier asset record identifies the artwork as the Burnout 3: Takedown icon
by diegou26 from [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com/icon/77642), originally
512 × 512, composited on white and resized to 1024 × 1024 on 17 October.
This restoration reuses the stored PNG byte-for-byte. The artwork and game
marks remain the property of their respective owners.
