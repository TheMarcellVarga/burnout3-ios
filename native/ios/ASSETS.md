# Artwork

`Assets.xcassets/GameLogo.imageset/GameLogo.png` is the original Burnout 3:
Takedown logo, downloaded unchanged from the Clear Logo (World) entry in the
[LaunchBox image collection](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/3938-burnout-3-takedown).
[Source PNG](https://images.launchbox-app.com/9f95331f-c20c-4e3a-9e7b-ddc94817872f.png),
1800 × 800 pixels, retrieved 2026-10-17. The original logo artwork remains the
property of its respective owners; it is not covered by the upstream code's MIT
license. It was used by an earlier startup screen.

`Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png` uses the official Burnout 3:
Takedown icon by diegou26 from [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com/icon/77642).
The source is a 512 × 512 PNG; it was resized to 1024 × 1024 and composited on
white to produce an opaque iOS app icon. Retrieved 2026-10-17. The artwork and
Burnout marks remain the property of their respective owners; they are not
covered by the upstream code's MIT license.

`Resources/game-title.webp` is the owner's supplied `b3-title-anim.gif.webp`,
copied unchanged on 2026-10-17. It contains four 600 × 420 frames, each lasting
2.5 seconds. The earlier title-screen launcher used view clipping to show its central racing/logo
panel, preserving the artwork's proportions, with native framing and the same
credits/prompt around it. It stopped animation when hidden, backgrounded or Reduce
Motion was enabled. Original game artwork and credits retain their ownership.

The previous universal launcher used `Resources/game-cover.jpg`, the original
North American PS2 front cover from the same LaunchBox collection. Source:
https://images.launchbox-app.com/58b3d988-8d18-4f56-a1c3-d87ca0808918.jpg
Downloaded unchanged on 2026-10-17. Original artwork retains its ownership.
The previous animated title art remains historical source material and is no
longer displayed by the launcher or included in the resource list for fresh builds.

## Direct game startup

The current startup flow uses a native ISO import screen on first use and starts
the saved game automatically afterward. The previous cover and game metadata
resources are no longer included in fresh app builds. Historical artwork above
is retained as source material.
